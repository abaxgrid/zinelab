How it works

  1. Lay outPick the paper and the number of pages, add your JPG photos and drag them into reading order. Add text, colors, patterns and vignettes.
  2. PreviewFlip through the zine on screen as spreads or single pages to check the sequence.
  3. PrintExport the PDF and print it at 100%. Print a test sheet on plain paper first to check the folds.
  4. FoldMaze zine: crease the sheet, cut the slits, then refold page by page. Booklet: fold each sheet in half, nest them and staple on the fold. The PDF includes the steps.

Photos stay private: they are kept in your browser and, when you save a project here, in your own claude.ai storage. RAW files need exporting to JPG first.

Most home printers need a margin of at least 5 mm. The white border frames each photo, like the reel.

Every second strip ends up upside down once folded, so those pages print turned 180° on purpose.

Two-sided flip

Home printers often place the back 1–3 mm off. Print a test sheet, hold it against a window, and compare the small crosshairs in the margins. Looking at the back side: if its crosshair sits 2 mm to the right of the front one, enter −2 left/right; if it sits too low, enter a negative up/down value.

Match your printer's two-sided setting. The back is laid out mirrored so each back page lands behind its front page. Use double-sided paper: most glossy photo paper prints on one side only.
